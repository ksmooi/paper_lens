Attention Mechanism

Bahdanau (2015)

  • BLEU En→Fr: 26.75 (RNNsearch-50)
  • Training: ~5 days, single GPU
  • Encoder: BiRNN (GRU, 1000 units)
  • Decoder: RNN + attention
  • Attention: Additive (concat)
  • Parallelization: Limited by RNN

Transformer (2017)

  • BLEU En→De: 28.4 (big) / En→Fr: 41.8
  • Training: 3.5 days, 8× P100 GPUs
  • Encoder/Decoder: Self-Attn + FFN ×6
  • Attention: Scaled Dot-Product
  • Multi-Head: h=8, d_k=64 each
  • Parallelization: Fully parallelizable

關鍵創新

  • No RNN — pure attention mechanism
  • O(1) sequential operations vs O(n)
  • Multi-Head: different subspaces
  • Sinusoidal positional encoding
  • 23× cheaper than ConvS2S Ensemble
  • Foundation for GPT, BERT, ViT, etc.
